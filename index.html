▶ ClipForge

ClipForge is a personal desktop tool. It runs on its owner's Windows PC, turns the owner's own gameplay recordings into short vertical videos, and, when the owner approves one, uploads it to the owner's own YouTube channel, Instagram account or TikTok account.

It is not a public service: there are no user accounts, no website login, and no data is collected from anyone else.

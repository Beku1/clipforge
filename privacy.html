ClipForge Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026

ClipForge is a personal desktop application that runs only on its owner's computer. It is used by one person, its developer, to upload their own videos to their own accounts. This policy explains what it does with data from Google (YouTube), Instagram and TikTok.

What ClipForge accesses

  • YouTube (Google sign-in): permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read the connected channel's name and ID (youtube.readonly), so it can show which channel is connected.
  • Instagram: the connected account's ID and username, and permission to publish reels to it.
  • TikTok: the connected account's display name, and permission to send videos to its drafts inbox.

How it is used

Only to upload videos the owner has approved, to the account the owner chose, at the time the owner chose, and to show the owner which accounts are connected. ClipForge does not read, analyze, or download other content from these accounts.

Where it is stored

Sign-in tokens and account names are stored only on the owner's computer. They are sent only to the platform they belong to (Google, Meta or TikTok) when uploading. ClipForge has no server, no analytics and no advertising, and it never sells or shares data with anyone.

Removing access

Removing a channel in ClipForge deletes its stored tokens from the computer. Access can also be revoked at any time from the platform: for Google, at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube

ClipForge uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

The developer's contact email is shown on the Google sign-in screen for ClipForge.
